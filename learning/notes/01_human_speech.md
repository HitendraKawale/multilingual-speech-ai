## What is human language?

  Human language is structured symbolic way of communication humans use. It relies on combination of vocab and grammar to formulate complex thoughts.
  
### Key characteristics

- Compositionality & Productivity: finite word infinite possibilities
- Displacement: can talk about abstracts that aren't actually happening or there
- Recursion: Sentences can be embedded into sentences without any upper limit
- Modality Independence: other mediums than being spoken can be written etc.

### How we see languages?

- Evolutionary way
- Sociological way
- Formal/Computational way

## What is Speech?

Speech is a vocalised form of human language.

## Speech vs Language

- Speech is physical: needs muscle, nerves, etc.
- Language is mental: needs grammar, vocab, meaning, etc.
- Independence: Sign Language is language without speech

## Six layers of Language

- Phonetics: physical sounds eg. where is the tongue?, how much air released?, etc.
- Phonology: rules for combining sounds. accents, dogs(z)
- Morphology: how words are built. un + happy + ness.
- Syntax: the dog chased the cat vs the cat chased the dog.
- Semantics: the bank is closed. river bank, money bank?
- Pragmatics: Its cold. could mean close the window, turn on the heater, etc.

## How does human conversation compare to ai voice assistant pipeline?

The functional architecture: Biological vs digital

[Human]       👂 Ear & Auditory Cortex  ===============>  🧠 Brain (Unified Network)  ===============>  🗣️ Vocal Tract & Muscles

[AI Loop]     🎙️ Mic -> VAD -> STT  ===============>  🤖 LLM (Text Processing)  ===============>  🔊 TTS Engine -> Speaker

- Human Conversation: Occurs as a holistic, native audio-to-audio experience. Your brain processes the acoustic pitch, emotional sentiment, and semantic meaning all at once, planning what to say while the other person is still finishing their sentence

- The AI Pipeline: Operates by converting mediums.
  - Voice Activity Detection (VAD): Listens to find out when you start and stop talking.
  - Speech-to-Text (STT): Translates your spoken sound waves into written text string variables.
  - Large Language Model (LLM): Ingests that text, calculates probabilities, and writes out a text-based response.
  - Text-to-Speech (TTS): Reads the LLM's text output and generates a synthetic audio wave to stream back over your speaker.
