## Difference between Phonetics and Phonology

Phonetics is the study of the physical properties of speech sounds.
Phonology is the study of the rules that govern the production of speech sounds.

## Speech Production

airflow ==> vocal cords ==> vocal tract ==> sounds

## why do we convert audio into spectrograms?

- spectrograms are a visual representation of the frequency content of a signal
- spectrograms are useful for understanding the structure of speech
- spectrograms are used in speech recognition and speech synthesis
- spectrograms are also used in speech enhancement and speech degradation

## Difference between amplitude and frequency

amplitude is the strength of the sound wave
frequency is the number of cycles per second
